# Observability analysis

Trace logging, metrics, tracing, alerts, and health checks from instrumentation to export/configuration. Recognize systems such as Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Jaeger, Zipkin, and ELK without assuming the full pipeline exists.

Record what operation is observable, labels/fields and correlation identifiers, failure coverage, sensitive-data handling, and operational use. A logging library proves logging capability only; dashboard/alert artifacts or explicit user evidence are needed for operational claims.
